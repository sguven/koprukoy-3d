# Street View tour notes (Apr 2026 imagery) — model coords are pin-frame (x east, z south, m)
01 market_junction looking N (214,187): Güvendioğlu Market = 2-storey, cream/white plaster, red-brown trim, ground-floor shop with awning and goods outside, big old plane/mulberry tree in front on the pavement; blue-white kerb on E side of the main road; wide asphalt junction (light grey, worn); çardak (gazebo) visible N-W across the junction; single-storey ochre building W side; minaret of Yeni Cami on the horizon N; utility poles + overhead cables along the E pavement.
02 market_south looking S (214,200): main road is light worn asphalt, 7-8 m, blue-white kerbs on E side; E side: single-storey long white building with hip tile roof set back behind a low fence, tall pines (karaçam) and a bare poplar in its yard; a small children's playground (orange slide) behind a low wall on the E pavement; W side: red-brown garden wall, bare trees, parked cars on gravel; utility pole with 6 cables W side. Houses S are 1-storey hip tile.
03 square_west looking W (195,182): narrow concrete/asphalt lane (3.5 m) running W from the square between: L = old stone-rubble garden wall (reddish rubble, ~1.2 m) with a white 1-storey house behind; R = tall cypress/juniper and a 2-storey white house with red trim and a shallow roof; further W 1-storey tile houses, tractor trailer in a yard, utility poles. Lots of mature trees over the lane.
04 mosque looking E from the main road (243,236): confirms v0.8 mosque: turquoise lime wash, one lead dome on a low drum, small windows, one slender beige stone minaret with a single şerefe, courtyard wall red-plastered ~1.8 m with concrete coping, lawn + picnic table + brick-paved pavement + blue-white kerb between wall and road; pines in the yard; bare mulberry at the wall.
05 main road N of market (205,60) looking S: junction with a steel lattice electricity pylon/transformer; chain-link fences around green plots; two-storey hip-roofed houses (cream/pink); long single-storey commercial building (blue shutters) at W side; TWO minarets visible to the SE (a second, twin-minaret mosque east of the main road — Fatih Camii); road here is unpaved/gravel at the junction, asphalt beyond.
06 N of village (150,-10) looking NW: typical older houses: 1-storey, white/pale-pink lime wash, low-pitch tile roofs with wide eaves, solar water heater on the roof, white rendered garden wall ~1.8 m with a steel pergola frame, green wire-mesh fence with steel posts along the lane, fruit trees in blossom, small pine.
07 E lane (360,90) looking NE: unpaved earth/gravel lane 4-5 m, no kerbs; R = dry-stone rubble wall (~1 m, granite boulders) along a garden, adobe/earth barn with sheet roof; L = wire fences, poplars; houses beyond 1-2 storeys; a 3-storey ORANGE apartment-like building further NE on the hill (prominent); rocky granite hill behind the village to the N.
08 school street (468,628) looking E: gravel/earth street 5 m with grass verges; R = 1.2 m cut-stone (ashlar granite) wall along the school/garden, big willow-like tree, red-brown 2-storey building (school) behind; L = 2-storey white house (flat-ish hip roof), stone rubble wall; granite hill (kayalık) closes the view E.
09 pool/park area (368,540) looking SE: wide gravel road, blue-white kerbs both sides, concrete-block rendered wall ~1.6 m with steel gate; white 2-storey hip house with barred windows; big cedar (sedir) in the yard; 3-storey cream building with balconies behind; many overhead cables; concrete utility pole with lamp.
10 S edge near health centre (560,860) looking E: open grassy common with grazing cows, chain-link fenced plots, earth track; granite ridge (kayalık tepe) immediately N/E of the village; 2-storey houses incl. a salmon-red one; concrete poles with lamps.
11 D260 looking NW from the Köprü Et Balık junction (-95,380): modern dual carriageway, black asphalt, white edge lines, central NJ/guardrail, 2 lanes per direction, wide gravel shoulders; granite outcrop hill with a red-roofed restaurant (Köprü Et Balık) at its foot; poplars/willows by the river on the left.
