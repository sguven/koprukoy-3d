# Köprüköy 3D

A 3D model of Köprüköy (Keskin, Kırıkkale, Türkiye) and the area around the Çeşnigir Bridge that you can explore in the browser.

**Live site:** https://sguven.github.io/koprukoy-3d/

- **Orbit, Walk, Fly and Toros** modes. Toros mode lets you drive a 1990s Renault 12 Toros station wagon. Touch controls work on phones.
- **Terrain:** SRTM 30 m elevation data; the lake (Kapulukaya Reservoir) sits at 724 m.
- **Roads, buildings and ground cover:** extracted from satellite imagery (v0.6 survey). Road centrelines and widths were measured from the road surface, and the top-down render was checked against the satellite image.
- **Çeşnigir Bridge (13th century, Seljuk):** 110 m long, 6 m wide, 18.60 m central span, 12 arches (9 lower, 3 upper). The lower arches are partly under the reservoir water.

- **v0.8 square and mosque:** the old municipality building with the Atatürk statue on its earth square, and the turquoise mosque with its single minaret and red courtyard wall, built from the Street View photographs in [`docs/reference/`](docs/reference/). A full re-layout of the centre from the satellite image is available behind `#village`. See [`docs/REALISM.md`](docs/REALISM.md).
- **v0.7 materials pass:** physically based, world-space textured terrain, buildings, roads, bridge, rocks, water and instanced ground cover. Textures are generated by `tools/gen_textures.py`.

Blender model: [`model/koprukoy.glb`](model/koprukoy.glb) (1 unit = 1 m, Y up, −Z north).

This is a draft. Storey counts and façade details are estimates.
