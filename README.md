# Köprüköy 3D

A 3D model of Köprüköy (Keskin, Kırıkkale, Türkiye) and the area around the Çeşnigir Bridge that you can explore in the browser.

**Live site:** https://sguven.github.io/koprukoy-3d/

- **Orbit, Walk, Fly, Toros and Game** modes. **Toros** drives a white Renault 12 Toros saloon through the village (chase, driver's seat and bonnet cameras, headlights, engine sound). **Game** (v0.9.6) is a Counter-Strike 1.5-style deathmatch against bots in the real village: walk controls plus a CS loadout — AK-47 / M4A1 / MP5 / AWP as the primary (Settings), Desert Eagle, knife; keys 1/2/3, Q, wheel, R reload, right mouse for the AWP scope, recoil and spread as in 1.5. 4–100 bots roam the real road network (lanes and junctions from the layout), stop and look around, spot you after a short reaction time, chase with run-and-gun, strafe at mid range, back off when you are too close, fall back to reload, run for cover when hurt, turn towards shots that hit them or that they hear; they carry the same rifles. 5-minute rounds, kills : deaths and a kill feed on the HUD, nearby bots on the minimap. Bullets and lines of sight are blocked by the houses and the terrain; everything is low-poly on purpose. See [`src/game.js`](src/game.js).
- **Map-style navigation (Orbit):** works like Google Maps — one finger drags the ground under it, two fingers pinch to zoom, twist to rotate and slide up/down to tilt; double-tap zooms in, two-finger tap zooms out. On desktop: drag, wheel, right-drag or Ctrl+drag to rotate/tilt. See [`src/mapnav.js`](src/mapnav.js).
- **Terrain:** SRTM 30 m elevation data; the lake (Kapulukaya Reservoir) sits at 724 m.
- **Roads and buildings:** Google Maps road layer and 0.3 m/px satellite imagery (v0.9 survey), georeferenced on the Çeşnigir Bridge; D.260 and the terrain are the v0.6 survey (SRTM).
- **Çeşnigir Bridge (13th century, Seljuk):** 110 m long, 6 m wide, 18.60 m central span, 12 arches (9 lower, 3 upper). The lower arches are partly under the reservoir water.

- **v1.12 smoother driving and minimap (Nov 2026):** the Toros stuttered because the whole shadow map (everything within ~140 m: ~400 draw calls and ~650 k triangles, 4096² on a computer, 2048² on a phone) was drawn again every frame while driving, only because the car casts a shadow and moves. Now the still world's shadow map is redrawn every 24 m, as in the Game, and the car alone is drawn on top of a cached copy of it each frame, in a small square around it ([`src/carshadow.js`](src/carshadow.js)); the shadows look the same (the car's shadow on the road, the roof shading the cabin, trees shading the car). The car's ~150 rigid exterior pieces are merged per material ([`src/toros.js`](src/toros.js), `mergeRigid`). Measured while driving the same lanes: draw calls 594 → 240, triangles 1.31 M → 0.73 M, shadow-map redraws every frame → 5 in 570 frames, main-thread time per frame 8.7 → 4.8 ms on a computer and 19.0 → 10.8 ms with a phone's slower processor emulated (walking is 3.4 ms). The minimap no longer jumps: it was redrawn into a canvas 30 times a second; now the map image is moved and turned with a CSS transform every frame, so it turns as smoothly as the view (Walk, Fly, Toros and Game). The speed box is written once a frame instead of in every physics step. See [`docs/PERF.md`](docs/PERF.md#v112-the-toros).

- **v1.1 Smoother game mode (Nov 2026):** the Game mode no longer stalls in the middle of a round. Every shader is compiled and every buffer and texture prepared while the page still says "Loading…", so the first look down a street, the first shot and the first bot no longer freeze a frame (benchmark, 50 bots: 10 shaders compiled during play → 0, longest frame 228 → 25 ms on the main thread). The sun's shadow map in the game is 2048² (phones 1024²), follows the player in whole texels and is redrawn every 24 m instead of every 6 m (166 redraws on the benchmark route → 33). The per-frame code allocates a quarter less memory (4.0 → 2.9 MB/s), bots think by distance (every frame within 60 m, ~20 times a second to 120 m, ~5 beyond) and animate their limbs only in view (100 bots: 0.69 → 0.38 ms of game logic a frame). On foot the eye height glides over kerbs and road edges instead of jumping (131 jolts on the route → 0), the mouse uses raw input where the browser offers it and turns slower through the AWP scope, as touch already did. New settings: look sensitivity and field of view (55–90°) under Controls, and a Game performance preset, Auto / Smooth / Quality. `#bench=50` (20 / 50 / 100 bots) runs a fixed route through the village and reports frame-time percentiles, draw calls, shadow redraws and more; the FPS meter also shows the p95 frame time and shadow redraws per second. The measurements were taken without a GPU, so the real frame times are still to be read on a laptop and a phone: see [`docs/PERF.md`](docs/PERF.md) for the tables, what each change bought and what was tried and dropped.
- **v1.0 Smoother frame rate (Nov 2026):** the scene no longer draws what the camera cannot see. Terrain, rocks, forest, the old buildings and the rebuilt village used to be one mesh per material spanning the whole map, so all ~1.9 M triangles were drawn every frame (and ~1.1 M again into the shadow map). They are now cut into map tiles ([`src/perf.js`](src/perf.js)), so the renderer skips tiles off screen; small village details (trims, railings, pipes, signs) are hidden beyond ~260 m (170 m on phones); grass and shrubs are drawn per patch and not at all beyond their fade distance. The sun's shadow map is redrawn only when the view has moved a few metres instead of every frame. Bots are drawn as instances (50 bots: ~66 draw calls in view → 7) and no longer cast shadows; bullet traces use a 20 m building grid (about 17× faster); the game HUD, kill feed and crosshair are written only when they change; the minimap redraws 30 times a second; switched-off headlights are left out of the shaders. An FPS meter (Settings › Performance › Show FPS) shows frame time, draw calls and triangles, and an automatic resolution steps the render resolution down (to 55 %) when frames are slow and back up when there is headroom; desktop renders at most at 1.5× pixel density.
- **v0.9.9 Game on phones (Nov 2026):** the Game mode is laid out for a phone browser. A Counter-Strike-style green crosshair with a dark outline is always shown in the middle of the screen (it used to be a faint white dot on desktop and hidden on phones); its gap opens with the real spread when you move or hold the trigger, and it hides behind the AWP scope.
