# Köprüköy 3D

A 3D model of Köprüköy (Keskin, Kırıkkale, Türkiye) and the area around the Çeşnigir Bridge that you can explore in the browser.

**Live site:** https://sguven.github.io/koprukoy-3d/

- **Orbit, Walk, Fly, Toros and Game** modes. **Game** (v0.9.6) is a Counter-Strike 1.5-style deathmatch against bots in the real village: walk controls plus a CS loadout — AK-47 / M4A1 / MP5 / AWP as the primary (Settings), Desert Eagle, knife; keys 1/2/3, Q, wheel, R reload, right mouse for the AWP scope, recoil and spread as in 1.5. 4–100 bots roam the real road network (lanes and junctions from the layout), stop and look around, spot you after a short reaction time, chase with run-and-gun, strafe at mid range, back off when you are too close, fall back to reload, run for cover when hurt, turn towards shots that hit them or that they hear; they carry the same rifles. 5-minute rounds, kills : deaths and a kill feed on the HUD, nearby bots on the minimap. Bullets and lines of sight are blocked by the houses and the terrain; everything is low-poly on purpose. See [`src/game.js`](src/game.js).
- **Map-style navigation (Orbit):** works like Google Maps — one finger drags the ground under it, two fingers pinch to zoom, twist to rotate and slide up/down to tilt; double-tap zooms in, two-finger tap zooms out. On desktop: drag, wheel, right-drag or Ctrl+drag to rotate/tilt. See [`src/mapnav.js`](src/mapnav.js).
- **Terrain:** SRTM 30 m elevation data; the lake (Kapulukaya Reservoir) sits at 724 m.
- **Roads and buildings:** Google Maps road layer and 0.3 m/px satellite imagery (v0.9 survey), georeferenced on the Çeşnigir Bridge; D.260 and the terrain are the v0.6 survey (SRTM).
- **Çeşnigir Bridge (13th century, Seljuk):** 110 m long, 6 m wide, 18.60 m central span, 12 arches (9 lower, 3 upper). The lower arches are partly under the reservoir water.

- **v1.0 Smoother frame rate (Nov 2026):** the scene no longer draws what the camera cannot see. Terrain, rocks, forest, the old buildings and the rebuilt village used to be one mesh per material spanning the whole map, so all ~1.9 M triangles were drawn every frame (and ~1.1 M again into the shadow map). They are now cut into map tiles ([`src/perf.js`](src/perf.js)), so the renderer skips tiles off screen; small village details (trims, railings, pipes, signs) are hidden beyond ~260 m (170 m on phones); grass and shrubs are drawn per patch and not at all beyond their fade distance. The sun's shadow map is redrawn only when the view has moved a few metres instead of every frame. Bots are drawn as instances (50 bots: ~66 draw calls in view → 7) and no longer cast shadows; bullet traces use a 20 m building grid (about 17× faster); the game HUD, kill feed and crosshair are written only when they change; the minimap redraws 30 times a second; switched-off headlights are left out of the shaders. An FPS meter (Settings › Performance › Show FPS) shows frame time, draw calls and triangles, and an automatic resolution steps the render resolution down (to 55 %) when frames are slow and back up when there is headroom; desktop renders at most at 1.5× pixel density.
- **v0.9.9 Game on phones (Nov 2026):** the Game mode is laid out for a phone browser. A Counter-Strike-style green crosshair with a dark outline is always shown in the middle of the screen (it used to be a faint white dot on desktop and hidden on phones); its gap opens with the real spread when you move or hold the trigger, and it hides behind the AWP scope. On touch screens the joystick appears wherever the left thumb lands (push it to the rim to run), the right side of the screen and the big FIRE button both aim when dragged (hold FIRE and steer), with RELOAD, JUMP and an AWP SCOPE button beside it; weapons are switched by tapping their names and a tap starts a new round. The HUD moves out from under the thumbs, keyboard hints and the disabled modes are hidden, notch and home-bar insets are respected, page zoom and bounce are blocked, a full-screen button appears where the browser allows it (Android, iPad), and the game renders at a lower resolution on phones to stay smooth.
- **v0.9.8 ÇEŞNİGİR-DER (Nov 2026):** the white house among the trees west of the çardak plot is the ÇEŞNİGİR-DER association building (Köprüköy Çeşnigir Kültür Sosyal Yardımlaşma ve Dayanışma Derneği): single storey, pale grey render, a glazed front under a flat canopy with the navy name board over the door and a string of Turkish flags, red tile roof with a yellow solar water heater. In front of it a paved forecourt with the çardak, tables and white chairs; a walkway from the main road with the flag pole and a twin street lamp; a raised garden terrace behind a blue-white railing. See [`docs/REALISM.md`](docs/REALISM.md).
- **v0.9.7 owner's corrections (Nov 2026):** on the lane NW of the big cemetery the front building is now the tandır house (adobe bake-house with a soot-blackened chimney and firewood), the dusty-rose house next to it has a full lower storey on its downhill west side and a single storey on the east, where the roof runs on over a terrace with a table and chairs and a grapevine pergola, the house behind is single storey, and a dry-stone wall runs along the lane. See [`docs/REALISM.md`](docs/REALISM.md).
- **v0.9.5 as Street View shows it (Nov 2026):** the village checked frame by frame against the 86 Street View panoramas and the Google Maps building layer. Fused 25–50 m "houses" are split into real footprints or a house plus a barn, one house in six is two storeys instead of one in three (none unconfirmed within sight of a panorama), façades carry 2–3 windows instead of a ribbon of six, footprints are off the road ribbons, road centrelines re-fitted to the lanes visible in the satellite image (the Google road layer was 10–20 m off in places), boulders are off the streets, mahalle lanes are earth and gravel, village asphalt is pale and dusty, garden walls whitewashed, plots green in spring. See [`tools/v095_fix.py`](tools/v095_fix.py), [`tools/shoot_pw.mjs`](tools/shoot_pw.mjs) and [`docs/REALISM.md`](docs/REALISM.md).
- **v0.9.4 checked against Google Maps (Nov 2026):** every road and building compared with the Google Maps map layer. The big cemetery south of the school is now one hillside cemetery (scattered graves and trees, rubble wall) instead of two small walled squares with houses around them; the two long buildings at its south end are the single-storey workshops of Ekinoğulları Demir Doğrama. The D.260 dual carriageway continues past the end of the viaduct along its real line (it used to stop and turn into a tangle of short road pieces at the junction). 124 "houses" that were really bare ground, graves, tarps, pools or the D.260 asphalt were removed and 71 buildings that Google shows but the model lacked were added; 9 dead-end stubs removed, one missing lane added. See [`tools/gmap_fix.py`](tools/gmap_fix.py) and [`docs/REALISM.md`](docs/REALISM.md).

- **v0.9.2 çarşı (Nov 2026):** the market square re-measured on the 0.25 m satellite photo and Street View 02/05: the Atatürk statue is back in front of the old municipality (bronze figure on a grey granite pedestal, grass bed, flag poles), the square is open pale gravel again (the traced road no longer cuts through it), and the municipality, Güvendioğlu Market and the kahve keep their measured footprints. Also: the kahve's green kiosk, the spruce and junipers in front of the façade, the raised park with the wooden çardak and blue-white kerb wall. See [`docs/REALISM.md`](docs/REALISM.md).

See [`docs/REALISM.md`](docs/REALISM.md) and [`docs/survey/`](docs/survey/).

A full re-layout of the centre from the satellite image is available behind `#village`. See [`docs/REALISM.md`](docs/REALISM.md).
